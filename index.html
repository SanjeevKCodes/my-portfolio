About

Hello! I am a recent graduate from Nepal Engineering College (NEC) (August 2024, CGPA 3.65/4.00). I now work on NLP and multimodal AI, and I am applying to research-based MS and PhD programs for Fall 2027.

My primary research interests are centered around:

  • NLP: How can we make NLP systems work for all of the world’s languages, including those with limited data? I am also interested in transfer learning, where models use knowledge gained from a source task for a target task.
  • Computer Vision: How can vision-language models (VLMs) detect image-text inconsistency and out-of-context images? I work with image processing and pattern recognition for this.
  • Multimodal AI: How can models jointly reason over text, vision, and speech?

Beyond these areas, I am also open to exploring Social Network Analysis and Multimodal Biomedical Informatics.
